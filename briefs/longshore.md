# Brief: Longshore Companion (timebook for ILWU longshore workers, account optional)

Read AGENT_BRIEF.md first: its tools, rules and final report all apply.
Only the goal changes.

- The app talks to its PRODUCTION servers. Never create an account, never sign in,
  never buy or start a subscription, never send a report or feedback form, and never
  use "Send it to a coworker". Decline every push-notification and purchase prompt.
  If a step needs an account, note it as a finding and go on signed out.
- Your goal, as your persona, signed out: pick a home local when asked (try Local 19
  Seattle or Local 13 Los Angeles), then
  1. read the Home screen (upcoming work, hall tape cards),
  2. open the Ships tab and look at Radar, Map and Coast,
  3. open the Timebook, add two shifts by hand (any day this week; pick a job from the
     job picker) and check the pay and hours shown,
  4. open Stats and Settings, and change one setting that matters to you (text size,
     theme, home local).
- Also look at an empty Timebook month, the job picker search, and any screen whose
  text is cut, overlaps or runs off the edge at your text size.
